Q: How does the "Watermarking" technique for LLMs work according to the research?
A: The "Watermarking" technique proposed in this research involves shifting the probability distribution of certain tokens (i.e., greenlist) to make the output text more distinguishable from that of an artificial intelligence model while still maintaining its fluency. The authors propose using a statistical method called conditional entropy to achieve this goal. Specifically, they calculate the conditional entropy of each token based on the entire input sequence and then shift it by subtracting the conditional entropy of the greenlist tokens. By doing so, the resulting output will have a higher conditional entropy than random noise, making it harder for humans or other models to identify as generated by an LLM.
