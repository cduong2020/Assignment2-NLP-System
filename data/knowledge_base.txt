RESEARCH PAPER: Title: Beyond Chain-of-Thought: Efficient Reasoning in Small Language Models
Abstract: This 2023 study from CMU LTI explores how models under 7B parameters can perform complex logical reasoning.
By using "Knowledge Distillation" from Llama-3, the researchers achieved a 15% increase in mathematical accuracy on the
GSM8K benchmark while maintaining low latency on edge devices.

RESEARCH PAPER: Title: Multilingual Alignment for Low-Resource African Languages
Abstract: Published in late 2023, this research focuses on improving translation for Swahili and Yoruba. The authors,
including faculty from the LTI, introduced a new dataset called "LTI-Africa-10" which contains 10,000 high-quality
sentence pairs. They found that "Cross-lingual Transfer Learning" is more effective than training from scratch.

RESEARCH PAPER: Title: A Watermark for Large Language Models
Abstract: As LLMs become common, distinguishing human vs. AI text is critical. This paper proposes a "Watermarking"
technique for LLM outputs. It shifts the probability distribution of certain tokens (the "Greenlist") to make the text
statistically identifiable as AI-generated without hurting fluency.

RESEARCH PAPER: Title: Social Intelligence and Ethics in Conversational Agents
Abstract: This paper by Maarten Sap and colleagues (2023) introduces the "SOCIAL-BIAS" framework. It evaluates if AI
agents exhibit microaggressions during long-form dialogue. The study concludes that current RLHF (Reinforcement
Learning from Human Feedback) methods often miss subtle cultural nuances.

RESEARCH PAPER: Title: OpenScholar: A Specialized LLM for Scientific Research
Abstract: Announced in early 2024 by CMU LTI, OpenScholar is a RAG-based system designed to help researchers synthesize
millions of papers. It uses a custom retriever to find "Gold Standard" evidence and provides citations for every claim made in its summary.
